How a Brisbane accounting firm added two tax accountants in six weeks

A 12-person suburban practice was turning work away every tax season. Here is how they added capacity without adding to the local hiring queue.

The firmSuburban accounting practice, Brisbane. Twelve staff, mostly small business and individual tax clients.
Roles hiredSenior Tax Accountant and Intermediate Tax Accountant
Time to startSix weeks from first call to first day
SetupWork from home in Quezon City, working Brisbane hours

The challenge

The firm had advertised for a tax accountant three times in a year. Each round brought a handful of applicants, two interviews and no hire. Meanwhile the two partners were reviewing returns at night and the senior accountant was carrying a lodgement list that kept growing. Growth was not the problem. Capacity was.

What Bravo did

  1. A 30-minute call to map the roles: what the partners wanted to stop doing themselves, the firm's software (Xero and Xero Practice Manager), and the hours.
  2. A shortlist in nine business days. Five candidates, each already tested on Australian individual, company and trust returns.
  3. Interviews run by the firm. The partners interviewed four candidates over two days and chose two.
  4. Employment and onboarding by Bravo. Compliant Philippines contracts, equipment, and a first-week plan agreed with the firm's senior accountant.

The outcome

2tax accountants added in six weeks
60%lower cost than the firm's last local hire, all in
0evenings of partner review by the second BAS quarter

Six months on, the senior accountant reviews rather than prepares, the partners have their evenings back, and the firm has taken on new clients for the first time in two years. Both team members are still with the firm.
