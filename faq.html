FAQ

What is your end-to-end process?

You make an enquiry and we have a short call about the role, your software and the hours you need. We search our tested pool and send a shortlist, usually within about 10 business days. You interview the people you like and choose. Bravo employs your team member in the Philippines, handles onboarding, and stays on hand after they start. See How We Work.

Where are your people based?

The Philippines. Our people team and office are in Quezon City, and team members work from home or from our office. The business is managed from Brisbane.

Can't I just hire offshore myself?

You can. What you take on is foreign employment and payroll compliance, screening for Australian experience, managing performance remotely, and finding a replacement if it does not work out. Bravo does all of that, and every candidate is checked on Australian work before you meet them.

How do your fees compare?

You pay the salary plus a transparent monthly management fee, invoiced in AUD. Typical all-in cost for an experienced bookkeeper or accountant is $3,000 to $4,500 a month. We show you the exact breakdown before you commit to anything, so you always know what your team member is paid.

Why not use a freelance platform?

Freelancers juggle several clients, are rarely tested on Australian tax or software, and move on. A Bravo team member is full time, employed properly, works your business hours, and works for you alone.

Aren't you taking jobs away from Australians?

Most of our clients come to us because they could not fill the role locally. Adding capacity offshore usually means the Australian team can take on more clients and more senior work, not less.

How are your people treated?

Every team member is employed by Bravo under a compliant Philippines contract with statutory benefits, paid fairly, and works daytime hours because they work the Australian day. You see what they are paid. We do not hide the salary inside a bundled rate.

How is Bravo different from a large BPO?

We are a small senior team, not a floor of hundreds. You interview and choose your own team member, they work for you alone rather than across several accounts, and the salary and our fee are shown separately. When something needs fixing, you call someone who knows your account.

Are they employees or contractors? Do we need to pay super?

Bravo employs your team member in the Philippines under a local employment contract. You engage Bravo under an Australian services agreement, so there is no Australian payroll, superannuation or PAYG for you to run. You receive one monthly invoice.

How do we pay?

One invoice a month, in AUD, covering salary and the management fee.

What hours do they work?

Your business hours. The Philippines is two to three hours behind the Australian east coast depending on daylight saving, so your team member works the Australian day.

How quickly can we start?

A shortlist usually takes about 10 business days. A start date is typically within 30 days of engagement, depending on the candidate's notice period.

What if the candidate doesn't work out?

In the first 90 days we replace them at no cost. After that we are happy to re-recruit for you. Ask us for the current terms.

Is there a minimum contract?

We explain the term and notice period upfront in your proposal, so there are no surprises. Ask us for the current terms.

How do you handle security and compliance?

Team members sign confidentiality agreements and work through your own systems and practice management software rather than saving files locally. We can also arrange secure devices and background checks. Talk to us about the setup your firm needs.

We already have an offshore team with someone else. Can we move them?

Yes. Tell us about your current setup in an enquiry and we will explain how a transition would work.

Who are you?

Bravo was founded by Australian finance and accounting professionals who had hired offshore themselves and wanted a more transparent way to do it. We are a small senior team across Brisbane and Quezon City. Meet the team.
